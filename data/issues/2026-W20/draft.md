# AI in Focus -- 2026-W20
*Weekly Intelligence Brief for the CFO & Finance Leadership Team | 15 May 2026*

This week, agentic AI crossed from pilot into production across the enterprise finance stack: SAP, KPMG, and Google each shipped deployments that put AI agents directly inside ERP and close workflows. The most material signal for finance teams is the arrival of ready-to-deploy, audit-grade agents for accounts payable, month-end close, and treasury -- removing the "not enterprise-ready" objection. On the risk side, foundation model API costs are rising sharply, and India's $9bn AI-native greenfield port raises the bar on what port customers will expect from operators in the next capital cycle.

---

## 1. Foundation Models

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.7 with major capability gains

**A materially more capable AI model is now available at the same price as its predecessor.** Opus 4.7 scores 87.6% on SWE-bench Verified (up from 80.8%) and 64.4% on the Finance Agent benchmark, the highest of any model, while vision accuracy jumped from 54.5% to 98.5%, all at the same $5/$25 per million token price. Finance teams building on Claude-powered platforms should revalidate performance benchmarks, as the capability improvement may unlock automation use cases previously below the reliability threshold.

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-4-7

OpenAI launched GPT-5.5 at double the API price

**Enterprises building on OpenAI APIs face an immediate cost review.** GPT-5.5 (API available April 24) doubled the price to $5/$30 per million input/output tokens, though OpenAI cites 40% token efficiency gains that bring the effective increase to around 20%. Teams should validate that claim against actual usage patterns before the next contract renewal.

Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-5/

## 2. Physical AI

India broke ground on a $9bn AI-native greenfield port

**A $9bn greenfield port designed with AI and AGV automation from the foundation sets a new benchmark for terminal competitiveness.** India's Vadhavan Port in Maharashtra embeds digital twin simulation and autonomous guided vehicle (AGV) infrastructure into its design before the first container arrives, unlike legacy terminals that retrofit automation later. Existing operators should assess how their automation roadmaps compare in the eyes of shipping line customers selecting future port calls.

Source: https://www.theweek.in/news/maritime/2026/05/13/vadhavan-port-rotterdam-benchmark.html

## 3. AI in Finance

SAP ramped up autonomous finance agents for CFO teams

**SAP's finance automation agents are now production-ready for S/4HANA customers, with financial close and procurement agents available in Q2.** The Autonomous Finance suite, confirmed at SAP Sapphire this month, delivers 200+ agents powered by Anthropic's Claude across close, AP, spend management, and supply chain, with the full agent portfolio reaching general availability by Q3 2026. SAP customers should map the release schedule against their upgrade plan and prioritise the Finance Close agent for the fastest measurable return.

Source: https://www.cfodive.com/news/sap-ramps-push-autonomous-finance-agentic-ai/819929/

## 4. AI Agents & Applications

KPMG deployed an AI close assistant inside Workday

**Big-4-grade AI is now executing month-end close tasks end-to-end inside a major ERP platform.** KPMG's Ignite Financial Close Companion, built on Google Cloud Gemini Enterprise, sequences close checklists, flags discrepancies, and posts adjustments via natural language inside Workday at audit-grade traceability. Port operators on Workday should request a scoped demo; a named Big-4 implementation partner removes the two most common procurement objections.

Source: https://kpmg.com/us/en/media/news/kpmg-debutes-ai-digital-assistant-with-workday-googlecloud.html

Google Cloud launched an enterprise AI agent governance platform

**Google's new agent platform addresses the governance gap that has held most CFO offices back from approving agentic AI in finance.** The Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform adds cryptographic agent identity, an auditable Agent Gateway, real-time anomaly detection, and pre-built templates for financial analysis and invoice processing. Finance technology teams should include this platform in the next agentic AI vendor evaluation alongside existing options.

Source: https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/introducing-gemini-enterprise-agent-platform
