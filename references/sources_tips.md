## Tip format in draft.md
Add at the end of the draft, after all news sections:

```
---

## Tip of the Week

[One-line description of the tip -- e.g. "Use Claude to draft board commentary"]

[2-3 sentences: what to do, how to do it, what outcome to expect. Keep it
actionable -- a finance team member should be able to try it the same day.]

Source: [URL]
```

## What makes a good tip
- Directly usable by a finance, treasury, or port ops professional
- Specific enough to act on (not "use AI to be more productive")
- Tied to a tool or platform the audience likely has access to
- Ideally demonstrates something new that was not possible 6 months ago
