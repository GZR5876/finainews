## Key players to watch
- Kalmar, Konecranes, ABB (port cranes & AGVs)
- Boston Dynamics, Agility Robotics (humanoid/industrial robots)
- Mobileye, Waymo Via (autonomous logistics vehicles)
- Windward, Navis, TBA Group (port operating system + AI)

## What to look for
- Autonomous terminal equipment deployments or contracts (AGVs, automated stacking cranes)
- AI-driven berth/vessel scheduling tools going live at peer ports
- Drone inspection programmes replacing manual surveying
- Incidents or recalls that signal safety/liability risk for operators
- Regulatory approvals or restrictions on autonomous port equipment

## Scoring notes
Score highest when tied to a named port deployment, quantified productivity gain,
or a capital procurement event. Generic robotics R&D scores lower unless it has
a clear port logistics application within 2 years.
