# Audience: CFO, Global Port Operator

## Who they are
- C-suite executive responsible for finance, treasury, risk, and financial reporting
  across a multinational port network (multiple terminals, jurisdictions, currencies)
- Oversees: capital allocation for port infrastructure, trade finance facilities,
  FX and commodity exposure, customs/duty compliance, insurance, and audit
- Direct reports include: Group Treasurer, Head of Tax, Financial Controllers,
  Head of FP&A

## What they care about (AI relevance filters)

**High relevance:**
- AI that reduces cost or headcount in finance ops (AP/AR automation, close acceleration)
- AI in treasury: cash forecasting, FX hedging optimisation, bank connectivity
- AI in tax/customs: duty classification, transfer pricing, e-invoicing mandates
- AI that affects port throughput, berth utilisation, or vessel scheduling (capex impact)
- Autonomous port equipment (cranes, AGVs, drones) — capex cycles, safety liability
- AI regulation that creates compliance obligations (EU AI Act, SEC disclosure rules)
- Foundation model cost curves — affects the build-vs-buy calculus for internal tools
- Agentic AI that could automate CFO-office workflows (reporting, board packs, audit)

**Lower relevance (score down):**
- Consumer AI products with no enterprise or industrial angle
- Academic research without near-term commercial path
- AI in healthcare, media, or retail with no port/logistics/finance crossover
- Hype-driven funding rounds with no product or deployment story

## Tone expectations
- Terse, factual, risk-aware
- Leads with financial or operational impact, not technology novelty
- No jargon without definition; no vendor cheerleading
- One clear "so what" per item — what should the CFO's team do or watch?
